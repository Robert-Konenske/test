Rules

Treat All Members with Respect

  • Treat everyone with courtesy and respect. Harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks will not be tolerated. We're all here to have fun and enjoy the games together.

No Spamming or Flooding Channels

  • Keep discussions relevant to the channel topics. Avoid spamming messages, links, or unnecessary large blocks of text. Let’s keep conversations clear and enjoyable for all.

Keep Language and Content Appropriate

  • While friendly banter is encouraged, please keep things appropriate for all members. Avoid excessive swearing, offensive jokes, or sharing inappropriate content (NSFW).

No Trolling or Baiting

  • Deliberately provoking or antagonizing other members for reactions is not allowed. This includes toxic behavior, flaming, or any attempt to create unnecessary drama.

No Cheating or Exploiting

  • We expect all players to follow the campaign or event rules. Any form of cheating, exploiting game bugs, or using unfair advantages will result in removal from events.

Resolve Conflicts Constructively

  • If disagreements arise, handle them respectfully. If you can’t resolve an issue, contact an admin or myself to mediate. Don’t escalate conflicts publicly!

Follow Moderator Instructions

  • Admins are here to keep things running smoothly. If they ask you to stop certain behavior or take action, please comply.

No Advertising Without Permission

  • Sharing links or promoting other Discord servers, businesses, or channels is allowed only with prior approval from Myself.

Keep Private Matters Private

  • Don’t publicly share private information such as addresses, phone numbers, or sensitive personal details. We want to ensure a safe and respectful environment for everyone.

Have Fun and Stay Engaged!

  • We’re here to build a great community, so stay active, enjoy the events, and feel free to share feedback to improve everyone’s experience!
