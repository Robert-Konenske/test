Universal Total War Zone WH3 Campaign Rules ⚔️🏆

  1. General Conduct 📜

    • Save Peeking 🕵️‍♂️: Loading a save to play as another player's faction and gaining information you could not otherwise obtain is strictly prohibited. If a player suspects you've done this, they can make a private accusation and have your behavior monitored and investigated. Loading into a save and playing turns that haven't occurred in the multiplayer campaign is highly discouraged—don’t ruin your reputation by seeking unfair advantages.
    • Battle Difficulty ⚖️: Do not change the battle difficulty at any point once all players have begun loading into the campaign. This is a severe penalty and a flagrant act of cheating. No excuses regarding battle difficulty will be accepted, so double-check your battle difficulty every single time you load into the campaign!
    • Campaign Etiquette 🫂
      • Action Loops: 🔄 Repeating an infinite loop of actions within the same turn is not allowed.
      • Blocking Movement: 🚧 Do not block armies from crossing bridges, landing, or attacking by placing heroes.
      • Replacing Lords: Do not perform any actions while a player is replacing a lord.
    • Diplomacy Restrictions 🏳️: Diplomacy is generally unrestricted, but there are exceptions. Pre-arranged alliances before the campaign starts are prohibited, and players must always compete for victory, not assist others to win. Players helping others to win without playing for their own victory are heavily discouraged. Teams and solo players may communicate and make pacts with others if they wish, but remember—only one team can win the campaign.
    • Player Etiquette 💬: Yelling, cursing, or acting rude and antagonistic toward others will not be tolerated. If you have an issue with someone, contact me privately to resolve it. Bad behavior or poor etiquette is a punishable offense. Be respectful of others and have a good time.

  2. Ambush Rules 🏹

    • Ambush Discovery 🔍: If an ambush is discovered (whether successful or failed), it may not re-enter ambush stance in the same turn. Exception: The Beastmen encampment may be used again at the end of the turn (end of turn qualified as after all opposing players have completed their actions).
    • Ambush-On-Attack Factions ⚔️: If playing as a faction with ambush-on-attack mechanics, you may not cancel an attack to reroll the ambush chance with the same army twice.
    • Ambush Rule Addition No army may move in ambush stance.
    • Voluntary Ambush Removal: If an ambush is not discovered, and a player voluntarily takes their army out of ambush stance, this does not count as the ambush being discovered, and no notification will be sent to other players.

  3. Hero Limits & Movement 🛡️

    • Hero Limits ⚔️: A maximum of three (3) heroes are allowed in any single army. If a battle is fought and a player has more than three heroes in an army, they will be required to immediately withdraw the excess heroes at the start of the battle, before casting any spells, using any abilities, or inflicting any damage with those heroes. The offended party can choose which hero(s) must be withdrawn.
    • Campaign Movement & Stance 🎲: You may not use any method outside of faction mechanics to refill movement or change stance when you have insufficient movement. This includes moving twice in Underway if interception is denied.

  4. Battle Conduct ⚔️🏰

    @everyone

    • Reinforcements 🚶‍♂️: Do not use army abilities (such as crowd control or debuffs) on units entering the battlefield as reinforcements. Spell casting on reinforcing units is allowed. If a player uses any army ability on reinforcements, the battle will be paused, and the offended party may request a rematch. This rule applies to any kind of army ability, whether damaging or not, including those that slow or debuff incoming units.
    • Large Armies Control 🎖️: The 'Control Large Armies' box may not be unchecked without mutual consent from all players involved in the battle.
    • AI Control in Battles 🤖: Armies should not be given to AI control unless both players agree. However, if the opposing player only has one army, the player with multiple armies may give AI control to one of their armies if they choose.
    • Magic Waiting 🧙‍♂️: You may not delay a battle indefinitely to accumulate magic power.
    • Stalling & Kiting 🏃‍♂️: Stalling the battle by avoiding combat when unable to do damage (e.g., excessive kiting or running away) is prohibited. If the attacking player hasn't damaged his opponents army for 20+ minutes they will receive a warning and potentially a strike if necessary.
    • Re-fought Battles 🔄: If a previously determined battle must be re-fought after loading a save, players must attempt to recreate the original outcome.
    • Conceding Battles 🚪: Players may not concede a battle without first obtaining explicit consent from the opposing player, this includes during the battle as well as after the battle. This rule ensures that all players have a fair opportunity to pursue in-battle objectives, such as loot, quest requirements, or strategic advantages. Any concession without consent will be treated as a rule infraction.

  5. Horde Faction Elimination 🏴‍☠️

    If a Horde faction's allies have no remaining settlements, and the Horde faction is outnumbered by more than 100 total settlements among all remaining teams, the Horde faction is automatically eliminated. Vassalized territories do not count toward settlement totals. This rule encourages Horde factions to ensure their allies' survival and not extend the game past a winnable state for said Horde faction.

  6. Player AI Control 🎮🤖

    • Manual Battle Against AI: If a player initiates a manual battle against an AI opponent, up to 1 player from each opposing team may choose to join and combat that player in the battle. For each manually fought AI battle, one opposing player will be designated as the "Rival" for that match.
    • Rival Player Control: The "Rival" player must control the majority of units during the battle. No participating player may control more than 4 units per army involved. Purposefully throwing a match or sabotaging as AI is strictly prohibited and is a reportable offense. Do not participate in a manual AI battle if you cannot remain unbiased and play to the best of your ability.
    • Conceding Battles: Players may not concede a battle without first obtaining explicit consent from the opposing player, this includes during the battle as well as after the battle. This rule ensures that all players have a fair opportunity to pursue in-battle objectives, such as loot, quest requirements, or strategic advantages. Any concession without consent will be treated as a rule infraction.
    • AI Settlement Trading Restrictions: No trading the same settlement to the same AI more than once per turn! Players can no longer trade a settlement to an AI they will go to war with the very next turn. A minimum of 3 full turns before they can retake it should be observed. Meaning if a player gives a settlement to AI on turn 15, they can't retake that settlement until turn 19. Don't trade a settlement that isn't in your possession anymore, if you send a trade request about a settlement that changed ownership to another player do not accept.

      • Single Entity Limit:

      • No single Army can have more than 10 Tier IV single entity units. No single Army can have more than 6 Tier V single entity units. (The icon next to the unit)

        • White Line Camping:

        • White Line camping is not allowed in battle.
